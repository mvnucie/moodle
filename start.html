How to Use This Course

Welcome to your Moodle course. This page will help you understand how the course is organized and where to find your weekly work, assignments, grades, materials, and updates throughout the semester.

Quick Tip: Check this course in Moodle regularly. Many common frustrations come from missing due dates, not seeing updated grades, or not knowing where materials are posted.

1. Start with the weekly or unit sections

Most of the course content is organized by weeks or units. To stay on track, begin by opening the section for the current week or unit.

In that section, you should typically find:

  • an overview of what you are doing that week
  • readings, slides, videos, or other course materials
  • assignments, quizzes, discussions, or other graded work
  • due dates and instructions

Helpful habit: Look at the current week first, then glance ahead to the next week so you can plan your time.

2. Where to find assignments and due dates

All graded work should appear in Moodle. Open the current week or unit to find the assignments for that part of the course.

Before starting an assignment, be sure to check:

Instructions

Read all directions carefully before you begin.

Due Date

Confirm when the work is due.

Attachments

Open any files, rubric, or linked resources.

Submission Method

Check whether it is submitted in Moodle or completed another way.

3. Where to find course materials

Course materials such as readings, PowerPoints, videos, handouts, and links are usually posted inside the weekly or unit sections.

Look for items such as files, pages, URLs, web links, or embedded videos.

Good to know: If your instructor refers to something in class, Moodle is the first place to check to see whether it has been posted for you.

4. How to check your grades

You can check your grades in Moodle throughout the semester to track your progress in the course.

  1. Open the course
  2. Click Grades in the course navigation menu
  3. Review your scores, feedback, and any missing items

Important: If something looks incorrect or confusing, contact your instructor promptly so the issue can be clarified while the course is still in progress.

5. How communication works in this course

Instructors may communicate in several ways, including Moodle announcements, course messages, email, or in-class reminders.

Check Moodle regularly for updates, especially before and after class meetings. Announcements may include reminders, schedule changes, or instructions for upcoming work.

6. What a typical week may look like

1

Review the weekly overview or directions.

2

Open the readings, slides, videos, or other materials.

3

Complete the required activities or assignments.

4

Submit your work by the listed due date.

5

Check for feedback and grades after submission.

7. What to do if something seems missing

If you cannot find an assignment, due date, grade, or course material:

  1. Look again in the correct week or unit
  2. Check announcements or recent messages
  3. Review the syllabus or assignment directions
  4. Contact your instructor promptly for clarification

Do not wait until the last minute if something seems unclear. Reaching out early can help you avoid missed work or unnecessary stress.
