Turnitin & AI Guidance for Faculty

Find practical guidance for interpreting Turnitin reports, understanding AI-supported writing tools, responding fairly when concerns arise, and setting clear expectations for student use of artificial intelligence.

Turnitin supports review; it does not make the decision.

A Similarity score or AI writing indicator should not be treated as automatic proof of academic misconduct. Review the report in context, consider the expectations students received, and follow MVNU's Academic Integrity Policy when concerns remain.
