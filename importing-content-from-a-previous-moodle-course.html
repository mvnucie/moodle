Importing Content from a Previous Moodle Course

Use Moodle's Import feature to copy activities, resources, course sections, and other content from a previous course into a new course site.

Before You Begin

Importing course content does not bring over students, student submissions, grades, or other student data from the previous course.

Start in the new Moodle course site into which you want to copy content.

Identify the previous Moodle course you want to use as your source. In most cases, this will be the most recent version of the course that contains the content and organization you want to reuse.

Important: If you have already imported content into the new course, avoid importing the same content a second time unless you specifically intend to do so. Importing the same material again can create duplicate activities, gradebook items, or course sections.

Import Your Course Content

  1. Log in to Moodle

    Go to courses.mvnu.edu. Then open the new course site into which you want to copy content.

  2. Open Course Reuse

    From the toolbar at the top of the course, select More and then Course reuse.

  3. Select Import

    On the Course reuse page, select Import.

    Under Find a course to import data from, Moodle will display courses you have taught previously. Locate the course FROM which you want to copy your content.

    If the course is not listed, use the search box. A useful search format is:

    [course number]-[section number].[term]

    For example: BIB-1023-1.2025FA

    If you still cannot find the course you need, open the previous course in Moodle, go to Settings, and locate its Course full name or Course short name. Copy one of those exactly and use it as your search term.

  4. Select the Correct Source Course

    Select the circle next to the course you want to import from, then select Continue.

    Before continuing, make sure you have selected the correct course, section, and semester.

  5. Choose What You Want to Import

    If you want to copy the entire course, leave the import settings selected and choose Jump to final step.

    If you only want to copy selected activities, resources, or sections, choose Next instead. Moodle will display the contents of the source course so you can select exactly what you want to import.

  6. Allow Moodle to Complete the Import

    Moodle will copy the selected content into your new course.

    Depending on the size of the course, this can take anywhere from a few seconds to several minutes.

    When the import is complete, return to your course and review the imported content.

After the Import

Importing copies the content and settings from the source course. Review the new course and make any changes needed for the semester you are preparing.

Review and Update Your Dates

Moodle copies the activity dates from the source course into the new course.

If you are copying a course from a previous semester, those dates must be updated for the new semester.

If you are copying content between two sections being taught during the same semester, Moodle will copy the dates from the first section. If those same dates apply to the second section, no additional changes are necessary.

The easiest way to review and update dates throughout the course is Moodle's Dates report:

More → Reports → Dates

The Dates report displays dates for assignments, quizzes, forums, and other Moodle activities in one place and allows you to edit those dates directly from the report. This is much faster than opening and editing each activity individually.

Review the full report and update the dates that need to change before students begin working in the course.

Review the Course Page

Look through each section of the course and make sure:

  • activities and resources appear where you expect them;
  • nothing was accidentally duplicated;
  • old announcements, instructions, or semester-specific information have been updated;
  • activities students should not yet see are appropriately hidden; and
  • activities you intend students to use are present and accessible.

Review the Gradebook

Go to Grades → Gradebook setup and review the organization of the gradebook.

Check for:

  • duplicate grade items;
  • obsolete assignments or categories that are no longer being used;
  • unexpected gradebook items; and
  • point values and category totals that do not match your current course plan.

Reorganize or remove items as needed for your course.

Check Publisher and External-Tool Activities

Publisher integrations and other external tools often require additional setup after a course has been imported.

If your course uses Connect, Achieve, Aktiv, Lumen, or another publisher or external platform, open the activities you plan to use and confirm that they are connected to the correct course or section for the current semester.

If an external activity asks you to select, pair, or link a course, make sure you are connecting it to the current semester's publisher course rather than the previous one.

If you have questions about anything you see while reviewing the imported course, or would like help with dates, the gradebook, publisher integrations, or general Moodle organization, contact CIE. We are happy to help.

If Your Course Uses Turnitin

There are two types of Turnitin activities you may encounter after importing an older course.

Regular Moodle Assignments with Turnitin Enabled

A regular Moodle Assignment can use Turnitin for Similarity Reports while retaining all of the normal Moodle Assignment features.

These assignments import as regular Moodle activities.

After importing, use the Dates report to update their dates along with the rest of your course, and confirm that the assignments appear where you expect them in the course.

Turnitin settings can be found by editing the Moodle Assignment and expanding the Turnitin plagiarism plugin settings section.

Legacy Turnitin Assignment 2 Activities

Older Moodle courses may contain activities labeled Turnitin Assignment 2.

Turnitin Assignment 2 is a legacy activity type. New Turnitin Assignment 2 activities can no longer be created in Moodle, but existing ones can still be brought into a new course through Import.

When a Turnitin Assignment 2 activity imports correctly, Moodle creates a new Turnitin assignment for the new course automatically.

After importing a course that contains Turnitin Assignment 2 activities:

  1. Locate each Turnitin Assignment 2 activity that you intend to use.
  2. Open it and make sure it loads normally.
  3. Use the Dates report to update its dates for the new semester.
  4. Confirm that the assignment appears in the course and is available to students when intended.

If a Turnitin Assignment 2 Activity Does Not Copy Correctly

Some older Turnitin Assignment 2 activities can fail during the import process. This is especially likely with older assignments containing lengthy assignment instructions.

Signs of a problem include:

  • an expected Turnitin Assignment 2 activity is missing from the new course;
  • an assignment appears in the gradebook but there is no corresponding activity on the course page;
  • opening the activity produces a Turnitin or Moodle error; or
  • Turnitin displays a warning that the assignment is connected to another Moodle course or that there is a duplicate assignment.

If you encounter any of these problems, contact CIE. We can identify what happened and help determine the best way to repair or replace the activity.

In most cases, the best solution for a problematic legacy Turnitin Assignment 2 activity is to replace it with a regular Moodle Assignment with Turnitin enabled.

You can also choose to replace a working legacy Turnitin Assignment 2 activity with a regular Moodle Assignment whenever it is practical to do so.

Final Course Check

Before students begin working in the course, review the site from top to bottom and confirm that:

  • all required course content imported successfully;
  • activity and assignment dates have been updated;
  • the course sections are organized correctly;
  • the gradebook reflects your current grading plan;
  • Turnitin activities you intend to use are present and working;
  • publisher or external-tool links are connected to the correct current-semester course; and
  • there are no unintended duplicate activities, gradebook items, or sections.

Taking a few minutes to complete this review at the beginning of the semester can prevent problems later once students begin completing and submitting work.

Need Help?

If you have trouble importing a course or notice anything unusual afterward, email the Center for Innovative Education at cie@mvnu.edu.

We're happy to help with course imports, dates, gradebook organization, Turnitin, publisher integrations, and other Moodle questions.
