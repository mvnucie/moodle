How can I see who has or hasn’t participated in a Moodle activity?

Back to Moodle FAQ

The Course participation report shows whether Moodle recorded a selected type of action for each student in one activity during a chosen period. Use it to answer focused questions such as “Who hasn't opened this resource?” or “Who has no submission-related activity for this assignment?”

The report checks Moodle activity records. It can confirm that an action occurred, but it does not evaluate the quality of a student's work or prove that an assignment, quiz, or other activity was completed correctly or on time.

Run the Course Participation Report

  1. Open the Report

    Open the Moodle course, then go to:

    Reports → Course participation

  2. Choose the Activity and Time Period

    Use Activity module to select the activity or resource you want to check. The report examines one activity at a time.

    Under Look back, choose how far back from the current time Moodle should search. Select a period that covers the time when students were expected to use the activity.

  3. Choose Whom and What to Check

    Set Show only to Student. If the course uses groups, you can also limit the report to a specific group.

    Under Show actions, choose the action that matches your question:

    • View includes read-type actions, such as opening or viewing the selected item.
    • Post includes participating actions that create, update, or remove information in the activity.
    • All actions combines View and Post.

    Important: Post is Moodle's technical label for several kinds of activity. It does not mean only writing a forum post, and a Post result does not necessarily confirm a final submission or completed quiz attempt.

  4. Generate the Report

    Select Go. Moodle will list the students who match your filters and show whether it found any matching activity for each student.

  5. Message Students Who Have Not Participated

    Select the checkbox beside each student you want to message. To select everyone with a No result for the activity, action, and time period you chose, select Select all 'No' at the bottom of the report.

    Then, from the With selected users... menu at the bottom of the report, choose Send a message.

What View and Post Mean for Common Activities

The actions included under View and Post depend on the type of activity selected.

Assignments

View can include opening the assignment, viewing the student's submission, or viewing feedback.

Post can include creating, saving, updating, submitting, duplicating, or removing submission content. A Yes result confirms submission-related activity, but it does not necessarily mean that the student made a final submission. Use the assignment's Submissions page to confirm the student's current submission status.

Quizzes

View can include opening the quiz or viewing an attempt, attempt summary, or review page.

Post can include starting an attempt, autosaving or updating answers, and submitting an attempt. A Yes result confirms attempt-related activity, but it does not prove that an attempt was completed. Use the quiz's Results page to confirm attempt and submission details.

Forums

View can include opening or searching the forum and viewing discussions.

Post can include creating or updating a discussion or reply. It can also include subscribing to or unsubscribing from the forum or a discussion. Because subscription changes count, a Yes result is not always proof that the student wrote a discussion post.

Resources and External Tools

Files, Pages, URLs, and Books normally produce meaningful View activity only. Selecting Post for one of these resources will not normally identify student participation.

For an external tool or publisher activity, Moodle may record that the student opened or launched the activity, but work completed inside the external platform may not appear in the Course participation report.

Read and Use the Results

A Yes indicates that Moodle found at least one matching participation event during the selected look-back period. A No means that Moodle found no matching event for that student within the selected filters.

A No does not mean that the student has done nothing in the course. It only applies to the selected activity, action type, and time period.

Understand the Number Beside Yes

The number in a result such as Yes (10) is the number of matching participation events Moodle recorded. It is not the number of submissions, quiz attempts, or completed activities.

  • With All actions selected, the number combines repeated views with any create, update, or other Post activity. This can produce a high count.
  • For an assignment, Yes (2) under Post could represent creating or saving the submission and later updating or submitting it. It does not necessarily mean that the student submitted twice.
  • For a quiz, the Post count can include starting the attempt, autosaving or updating answers, and submitting. It should not be interpreted as the number of attempts.

Use the count as a general indication of activity. When the exact sequence or status matters, open the activity's own submission or results page or review the Moodle logs.

When This Report Is Most Useful

Use the Course participation report when you need a quick answer about one specific activity, especially to:

  • identify students who haven't opened an important reading, file, page, or assignment;
  • identify students with no submission-related activity for an assignment;
  • identify students with no attempt-related activity for a quiz;
  • screen for students with no Post activity in a discussion forum;
  • check participation during a recent period without reviewing individual students one at a time; or
  • select nonparticipants and send a targeted reminder.

It is not designed to show a student's complete sequence of actions across the course, confirm final submission status, or report configured completion status. Use it when the question is limited to whether Moodle recorded a particular kind of action in one activity.
