How can I see student progress with the Activity completion report?

Back to Moodle FAQ

The Activity completion report provides a course-wide view of the completion conditions students have met. It is most useful when you want to scan progress across several activities or identify students who may be falling behind.

The report includes only activities and resources for which Activity completion has been configured. If expected items are missing, see How do I set up Activity completion in my course?

Open the Activity Completion Report

Open the Moodle course you want to review. From the course navigation, use:

Reports → Activity completion

Moodle displays one row for each student and one column for each tracked activity or resource. In a large course, you may need to scroll horizontally to see all activities.

Read the Report

Each box shows whether a student has met the completion conditions for that item. The border also shows how completion is determined.

Completion Method

  • A dashed border means Moodle determines completion automatically from the conditions configured for the activity.
  • A solid border means the student can mark the item complete manually.

This distinction is easiest to see while the box is empty. Once the activity is complete, the green checked box replaces the visible border.

Completion Status

  • An empty box means the configured completion conditions have not been met.
  • A green checked box means all configured completion conditions have been met.

Depending on the activity's settings, completion might mean that the student viewed the activity, submitted work, received a grade, earned a passing grade, or met another configured condition.

Override a Completion Status

If a completion status needs to be corrected, select the relevant box in the report to mark the activity complete or not complete for that student. Automatically completed quizzes cannot be manually changed to not complete.

Important: Changing a box overrides the student's recorded completion status. Use an override only when you intend to change the official status, not simply to highlight or annotate the report.

Best Uses for the Report

The Activity completion report is especially helpful when you need to:

  • identify students who have not completed required work by a particular point in the course;
  • check whether students have completed prerequisite readings, videos, quizzes, or other activities;
  • review progress before contacting students who may need support;
  • compare completion patterns across several course activities; or
  • spot an activity that many students have not completed, which may signal that its instructions, availability, or completion conditions need review.

What the Report Does Not Show

A completion mark means only that the configured conditions were met. Depending on the activity, that might mean the student viewed a resource, submitted work, earned a required grade, or manually marked the item complete. It does not always indicate the quality of the student's work or the amount of time spent on it.

Use the gradebook when you need scores or grading details. Use other course reports when you need a detailed history of student actions or participation in a specific activity.
